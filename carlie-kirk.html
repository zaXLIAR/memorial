Carlie Kirk

October 14, 1993 - September 10, 2025

Legacy

A tireless advocate for social justice and equality in underserved communities.

Biography

Carlie Kirk dedicated her life to fighting for racial justice and equality. She worked extensively with youth programs and community organizations to create opportunities and advocate for systemic change.

Through her leadership in local activist groups, Carlie helped organize peaceful protests, educational initiatives, and policy reform efforts that continue to inspire the movement for social justice.
