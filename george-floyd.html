George Floyd

October 14, 1973 - May 25, 2020

Legacy

His tragic death sparked a global movement against police brutality and racial injustice.

Biography

George Perry Floyd Jr. was an African-American man who was murdered by a police officer in Minneapolis, Minnesota, during an arrest after a store clerk suspected Floyd may have used a counterfeit $20 bill.

Born in Fayetteville, North Carolina, Floyd was raised in Houston, Texas, where he was known as a talented athlete. His death led to worldwide protests against police brutality, especially towards Black people.
